# Scope

- Public IT roles with a German workplace or explicit remote eligibility for Germany.
- Repeatable manual imports, stable job IDs, updates, and closure tracking.
- Local `search` and `show` commands with plain-text and JSON output.
- Original job links, data provenance, and visible freshness.
- No paid data subscription.

Nationwide coverage is the long-term goal. The MVP reports its measured coverage
and known gaps.
